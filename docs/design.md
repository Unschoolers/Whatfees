# WhatFees marketing page

A mobile-first Vue marketing site for collectors who sell, with focused fee calculator, break-even and TCG inventory pages. Its primary homepage action opens the free calculator on the page; the secondary action opens https://app.whatfees.ca; Android links to the existing io.whatfees listing. English and French share layouts with separate, prerendered URLs and crawlable language links.

Use the app's cream, charcoal, gold and red palette, with bold collectible-inspired typography and clean marketplace spacing. Lead with understanding profit after costs and fees. Follow with inventory, pricing and sales; show real app screens using fictional demonstration data. Show real product proof early, explain Free/Pro access, and demonstrate wheel, mystery grid and bracket battles using clearly labeled interactive illustrations. Avoid claims of universal marketplace synchronization, fabricated testimonials or promised earnings.

Keep the site static, without accounts, analytics or a backend. Use GitHub Pages with www.whatfees.ca, leaving app.whatfees.ca unchanged. Verify responsive layouts at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels, both languages, keyboard navigation, FAQ disclosure, image loading and outbound destinations. Build production assets before handoff.

The portfolio scenario now opens on a native SVG chart with clearly labeled fictional CAD data, a break-even baseline and an accessible data table. The blank portfolio screenshot is no longer used.

Game illustrations preserve their state: wheel results appear after the spin, revealed mystery cells remain visible until reset, and the four-entry dice bracket advances through two semifinals and a final. Reduced-motion users receive results without animation. Wheel labels stay upright, and bracket connections align with each participant.

Keep copy specific to what the app does. The creator intro identifies Julien as a software developer and TCG seller through Jule’s Arena; it does not invent an origin story. Mark’s testimonial is supplied by the owner and remains in its original English wording in both languages. Pro is US$10 once, with the same Google account granting web and Android access. The privacy link points to the app policy. Only add a contact address supplied by the owner; an address appearing in another page is not enough confirmation.

The homepage calculator needs no account. Advanced fee, shipping and tax fields expand on demand and retain their values. Real screenshot crops prioritize populated sales data; selecting an image opens the full screenshot. Game illustrations show a read-only spectator panel driven by the same local demo state as the host controls.
